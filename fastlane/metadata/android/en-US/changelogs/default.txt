- New puzzle: Reckon. Fill a 6x6 grid with 1 to 6, once in every row and column, so the digits in each cage make its number with its sign: add, take away, multiply or divide.
- Its hints explain each cage's combinations, and a Training course drills them.
- Every puzzle now has the bright Arcade look; Classic keeps the calmer colours.
- Who's Who is played as a web of links: draw lines, rule pairs out, let the rest follow.
