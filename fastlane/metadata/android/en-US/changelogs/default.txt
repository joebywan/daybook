- Word Wheel's hints give the clue first, then a second clue and a letter, before the whole word.
- Shikaku shows the size of the rectangle you are drawing, beside your finger.
- Dragging on Shikaku, Thermometers, Tally, Tents, Kings, Lamps and LITS tints the row and column under your finger, so you can see where you are aiming past your hand.
- Cage Sudoku colours its cages, with bigger totals and bolder 3x3 lines, so the board is easier on the eyes.
