# Daybook privacy policy

Daybook collects nothing.

- The app declares **no `INTERNET` permission**, so it cannot send data anywhere even in principle.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
- Your puzzle progress, streaks and statistics are stored only on your own device, and are removed
  when you uninstall the app.
- Nothing you do in the app is transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.

Daily puzzles are generated on your device from the date. They are not downloaded, so the app never
contacts a server to fetch them.

If Android's own backup is enabled on your device, your progress file may be included in that
backup, which is handled by Google under their terms, not by this app.

## Web version

The web version at https://knowhowit.com.au/daybook/ works the same way. Your progress is stored in
your browser's local storage on your own device, and the game sends nothing anywhere. A backup is a
file you download yourself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep standard
access logs (such as IP address and browser), as any web host does; that is handled by GitHub under
their terms, not by this game.
