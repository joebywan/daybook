# Partner-word categories ("___ fish", "Fire ___") take a closed compound (catfish, fireplace) or a fixed phrase
# common in every dialect (fire engine, fruit fly, water bottle), never a phrase made up on the spot. "Hide a ..."
# means the smaller word sits inside the shown word as a run of letters and is not the whole word; crude readings
# (ass in bass, tit in petite, bum in bumper) are deliberately not counted. Membership is complete over this file's
# vocabulary: a word that fits a category here is listed under it, even when it was written for another.
