# Quartets bank: cross-tagging (docs/QUARTETS_SPEC.md, "The bank"). Each line adds members to a category of the same
# title and level in plain.txt or wordplay.txt (the tool merges equal titles), so the authors' files stay as they wrote
# them and every membership found by reading the whole bank's vocabulary against each category is in one place.
# A fit a player could reasonably argue for is added: an extra listed fit can only make the generator turn a board
# down, while a missing one would refuse a right answer.
#
# Fits nobody should be dealt as the answer are listed QUIET, as ~word: the proof and every board count them, so a
# player who argues for one is never refused, but the generator never deals one as its category's four, and the tier
# gate does not count a quiet fit as a spare or a phantom. Two kinds: grim or crude (Head + stone, Black + eye, see
# "Quiet memberships"), and real but obscure, technical or regional (moonfish, wardship, firebrick: "Second sweep").
