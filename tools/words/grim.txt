# The breakfast-table screen, one list for every word game (the owner, 2026-10-26: "one app-wide grim-word list
# across every word game"). A word here is never an answer or a printed grid word: not Lexicon's daily word, not a
# Word Wheel grid word or wheel, not a Codeword or Crossword grid word, not a word of a Cipher sum. It stays a legal
# guess and a Word Wheel bonus word: a player may still type it.
#
# The bar: a word you would not want as the daily answer, or printed in a grid, on the breakfast table. Death and
# killing, violence and weapons, bodily fluids and functions, disease that marks a person, drink and drugs, and
# words used to insult a body or a mind. An ordinary word with a grim second sense stays (die, hang, knife, drug,
# abort, gash, tomb, coma): do not over-screen. Crude and sexual words are in exclude.txt; this list is for words
# that are clean English but grim.
#
# Read by tools/words/build.py, which takes these out of the answers (so out of every list the other games draw
# their words from), and by GrimWordsTest, which fails if any game shows one. A change here moves daily boards:
# rebuild every generated list (CLAUDE.md, Lexicon) and re-pin BoardPinTest.
#
# Started from the Crossword's own list (MiniGridsBuildTest.SKIP, now gone), less die, hang, knife, drug and abort.
