# Mines
> Every safe square, found without a guess

## How to play

The first squares are already open when the board loads. The Reveal and Flag buttons under the board set what a tap does. A long press does the other one, and so does a double tap with Flag chosen.

Tap a number whose flags already match it to reveal all its other squares at once. A wrong flag there opens a mine and loses the board.

A flag is your own note: it is never checked, and it keeps its square from being revealed by mistake. Flag it again to take it off. The counter says how many mines are left, less your flags.

On a keyboard, the arrows move, Space or Enter reveals (on a number, reveals around it), and F flags or unflags.

There is no undo. Reveal a mine and the board is lost, and Try again starts it again from the opening.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me". Every board can be cleared without a guess.

## Rules

- Each number counts the mines in the squares touching it, corners included.
- Reveal every square without a mine. Reveal a mine and the board is lost.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: 8x8 field, 10 mines. Single numbers are enough.
- Hard: 8x10 field, 16 mines. Needs one number's squares inside another's, or two numbers that share squares, at least once.
- Expert: 9x10 field, 21 mines. Needs the numbers counted against the mines left, or a what-if, at least once.

## Techniques

These are the steps the hints teach, simplest first. Try them in this order. On each board, the dark squares are hidden, F is a flag, and a blank open square touches no mines. The tinted squares are where to look, rings mark what the reasoning leans on, and the filled squares are the move: ✓ a square to reveal, F a square to flag. Every board here is a real position from the game, and this is the step the hint gives on it.
