# Thermometers
> Fill each one from the bulb up

## How to play

Tap a square to fill its thermometer from the bulb up to there. Tap the top of the fill to lower it by one. Double-tap or long-press a square to cross it out, and everything above it in its thermometer.

Pick Fill or Cross under the board for what a single tap does. Drag along a row or column to mark several squares at once. A cross is only a note: a crossed square counts exactly like any other square left empty.

On a keyboard, the arrows move, F fills to the square, X crosses from it, Space cycles, and Backspace clears. Shift and an arrow carries a mark along.

A count turns green when its line holds that many filled squares. It turns red a moment after the line can no longer get there: too many filled, or too few squares left open. A thermometer's outline turns red if a filled square sits above a cross.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me". If a mark you made is wrong, Hint points at that first. Every board has exactly one answer, and you never need to guess to find it.

## Rules

- Each thermometer fills from its round bulb, with no gaps: a filled square has every square below it filled.
- The number beside each row, and under each column, is how many of its squares are filled.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: 5x5 grid. Lines and thermometers that can't reach the top are all it needs.
- Hard: 6x6 grid, longer thermometers. Needs a bulb that must fill at least once.
- Expert: 8x8 grid, longer thermometers still. Needs a what-if at least once.

## Techniques

These are the steps the hints teach, simplest first. Try them in this order. On each board, a thick line is a thermometer and the o is its bulb; a dark square is filled, an x is crossed out, the numbers in the margin are the counts, the tint is where to look, a ring is what the step leans on, and the filled square is the mark to make next. Every board here is a real position from the game, and this is the step the hint gives on it.

### from-the-bulb | A thermometer filling from its bulb

A thermometer has no gaps. Under a filled square everything is filled, down to the bulb; above an empty square nothing can fill.

### tip-cannot | A tip that cannot fill

A thermometer fills from its bulb, so its squares in a line fill in order. If a line needs only k more, one thermometer can give it at most k: its next open square in that line, and everything above it, stay empty.

~~~board

. . . o . 3
o o o . . 3
. . . . . 1
. . o o . 3
1 3 3 3 1 _
~~~

### what-if | A what-if
