# Reckon
> Make every cage's number, 1 to 6 once per line

## How to play

Tap a square, then tap a digit. Tap the digit again to clear it.

Or pick the digit first: with no square selected, tap a digit, then every square it goes in. Tap the selected square to let go of it, and the digit again to finish.

Tap the pencil to take notes. While it is lit, a digit is pencilled small in the square instead of placed, and placing a digit clears it from the notes of the squares in its row and column.

On a keyboard, the arrows move, 1 to 6 place a digit (or a note in pencil mode), and Backspace clears.

A repeat in a row or column, or a cage that can no longer make its number, turns red after a moment, but nothing stops you placing a digit.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. You make the move yourself, unless you ask for "Show me". If a digit you placed is wrong, Hint points at that first. Every board has exactly one answer, and you never need to guess to find it.

## Rules

- Fill every square with a digit from 1 to the size of the grid: no repeat in a row or a column.
- Bold lines mark cages. The number and sign in a cage's corner is what its digits make: added (+), multiplied (×), or in a cage of two, the bigger less (−) or over (÷) the smaller.
- A cage may repeat a digit when the two squares share no row or column.
- A cage of one square is given, and stays put.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: Cages of up to 3 squares, a few digits given. About 18 cages, 3 to 5 of them single given squares.
- Hard: Cages of up to 4 squares, one or two digits given. About 15 cages. Needs a cage that settles a digit for its whole line, or two squares sharing two digits, at least once.
- Expert: Cages of up to 5 squares, no digits given. About 13 cages, and the cages are all you start from. Needs a what-if at least once.
