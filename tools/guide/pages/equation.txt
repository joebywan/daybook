# Equation
> Find the hidden equation

## How to play

Type an equation on the keys under the board and press Enter. A physical keyboard works too: type digits, + − * / (or X for ×) and =, Backspace deletes, Enter submits.

Each symbol of a guess is then marked:

- Green: the right symbol in the right place.
- Yellow: the symbol is in the equation, but somewhere else.
- Grey: the equation has no (more) of that symbol.

A symbol is marked only as many times as the equation holds it, so a second 1 can be grey while the first is yellow. The keys keep the best mark each symbol has had.

Every guess must be a true equation of the right length, with one = and a single number after it. × and ÷ come before + and −, every ÷ comes out whole, and no number starts with 0. A row that isn't true is refused with the reason (for example "Not true: the left makes 15"), and doesn't use up a guess. Any true equation is allowed, even one the marks have already ruled out, and that can be a good way to test several symbols at once. The hidden equation never has a number that is just 0, and never multiplies or divides by 1.

Stuck? The first tap on Hint says where to look, and the second explains why. A hint that points at an equation gives its shape (its signs and the number after =, the other digits as ?), and only "Show me" types it.

## Rules

- Find the hidden equation before you run out of guesses.
- Every guess must be a true equation of the right length.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: Six symbols, six guesses.
- Hard: Seven symbols, six guesses.
- Expert: Eight symbols, five guesses. Far more equations fit eight symbols, so there is more to work out after each guess, and one guess fewer to do it in.

## Techniques

These are the steps the hints teach. On the board, a green tile has an inner line and a yellow one a corner dot.

### opener | A good opening equation

Nothing is known yet, so start with an equation of many different digits and signs: it cuts the list down fast. The hint names one, and which one changes from board to board. It says nothing about the hidden equation.

### best-guess | The equation that narrows it most

When the marks force nothing, pick an equation that fits them and splits what's left. The hint reads the list of equations it could be here, says so, and tells you how far its pick narrows things down, given as its shape.

### pinned-symbol | A symbol pinned to its slot

You know a symbol is in the equation. Cross off each slot a mark has ruled out for it and each slot already held by a green. If only one slot is left, that's where it goes.
