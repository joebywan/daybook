# Trail
> One line, every square, numbers in order

## How to play

Drag to draw the line, starting from 1. Lift off whenever you like and carry on from the end. Drag back along the line, or start again from a square on it, to rub it out.

Trail has no hints. Your first board opens on a walkthrough that teaches the reasoning, and the "?" sheet takes you back to it. Every board has exactly one answer.

## Rules

- Draw one continuous line from 1 to the highest number.
- The line must pass through every square exactly once.
- It must reach the numbered squares in ascending order.

## What changes per tier

- Standard: 4x5 grid.
- Hard: 5x6 grid.
- Expert: 6x7 grid.
